# Prove high-risk integrations before feature development

FormulaBridge does not begin broad feature implementation until executable spikes prove four architectural assumptions across the supported Word matrix: signed per-user VSTO installation and automatic loading, source-preserving ordinary copy, SVG plus PNG save/reopen/copy/print/PDF round trips, and TeX filesystem isolation. A failed spike causes the relevant architecture decision to be revised and revalidated rather than being deferred to late product testing.
